# APA 7th Edition — Quick Reference

> Compiled for the Rafiq research paper. Source authority: APA Style (apastyle.apa.org), Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association (7th ed.), and Purdue OWL. This is a working reference; verify any borderline case against the manual before final submission.

---

## 1. Core formatting

- **Font:** 12 pt Times New Roman, Calibri, Arial, or Georgia (one consistent throughout)
- **Spacing:** Double-spaced body. Single-spaced block quotes (≥40 words) and references
- **Margins:** 2.54 cm (1 in) all sides
- **Alignment:** Left, no justification
- **Paragraph:** First-line indent 0.5 in, no extra space before/after
- **Title page:** title (3–4 lines from top), author names, institutional affiliation, course, instructor, due date, page number (in header)
- **Page numbers:** top-right, all pages including title; running head on title page only (for student papers) or on every page (for professional papers)
- **Headings:** 5 levels, bold, with parallel structure across the manuscript
- **Voice:** Third-person predominant; first-person permitted when explicitly justified

## 2. In-text citation formats

### 2.1 Parenthetical (after the relevant idea)

| Source type | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| One author | (Author, Year) | (De Freitas, 2024) |
| Two authors | (Author1 & Author2, Year) | (Brandtzaeg & Følstad, 2022) |
| Three or more | (First author et al., Year) | (Hevner et al., 2004) |
| No author | (Title, Year) | ("AI Companions Reduce Loneliness," 2024) |
| Group as author, first use | (Full Name, Year) | (American Psychological Association [APA], 2020) |
| Group as author, subsequent | (APA, 2020) | |
| Multiple works, same point | (Author1, Year; Author2, Year) | (Hevner et al., 2004; Peffers et al., 2008) |
| Multiple works, same author/year | (Author, Year, Year) | (Hevner, 2004, 2007) |
| Personal communication | (J. Chen, personal communication, May 1, 2026) | never in reference list |

### 2.2 Narrative (as part of a sentence)

- "**De Freitas (2024)** found that…"
- "**Hevner et al. (2004)** propose seven guidelines for…"
- "**Brandtzaeg and Følstad (2022)** argue…"

### 2.3 Direct quotations

| Quote length | Treatment |
|---|---|
| ≤ 40 words | In quotation marks, page required: (De Freitas, 2024, p. 12) |
| > 40 words | Block quote: indented 0.5 in, no quotes; period before citation: p. 12. |
| No page numbers (e.g., web) | Use paragraph number: (Brandtzaeg, 2022, ¶ 4) or section heading |

### 2.4 Common pitfalls to avoid

- **No comma** between author and year in parenthetical: ` (Hevner et al., 2004)` ✓ ; `(Hevner, et al., 2004)` ✗
- **No "and"** in parenthetical; use `&`. Narrative uses "and"
- **"et al."** preceded by comma: `(Hevner et al., 2004)`
- **Always year** in the citation, even for very well-known works
- **Page number** required for direct quotes; optional for paraphrase unless quoting a specific passage

## 3. Reference list — formats by source type

Hanging indent 0.5 in. Alphabetical by first author's last name (or title if no author). Sentence-case for titles; italic for books, journals, and report titles.

### 3.1 Journal article (with DOI)

> Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of the article in sentence case. *Journal Name in Title Case and Italic*, *volume*(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxxx

Example:
> De Freitas, J., Uguralp, A. K., Uguralp, Z. O., & Stefano, P. (2024). AI companions reduce loneliness. *Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans*, *1*(3), 100007. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2024.100007

### 3.2 Book

> Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of book in sentence case italic* (Edition if not first). Publisher. https://doi.org/xxxxx (if applicable)

Example:
> Bowlby, J. (1969). *Attachment and loss: Vol. 1. Attachment*. Basic Books.

### 3.3 Edited book chapter

> Author, A. A. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor (Ed.), *Title of book in sentence case italic* (pp. xx–xx). Publisher.

Example:
> Dourish, P. (2001). Where the action is: The foundations of embodied interaction. In *Where the action is* (pp. 1–22). MIT Press.

### 3.4 Conference paper (published proceedings)

> Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of paper*. In *Proceedings of the Xth Conference on Y* (pp. xx–xx). Publisher.

Example:
> Kaltenhauser, A., Stefanidi, E., Schönbeck, T. J., Averin, K., Dingler, T., Memar, A. H., & Draxler, F. (2024). Playing with perspectives and unveiling the autoethnographic kaleidoscope in HCI – A literature review of autoethnographies. In *Proceedings of the 2024 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems* (Article 819, pp. 1–20). ACM. https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642355

### 3.5 arXiv preprint (treat as journal article equivalent; APA 7.1 example)

> Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of preprint*. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/xxxx.xxxxx

Example:
> Rhel, H., & Roussinov, D. (2025). *Large language models and Arabic content: A review*. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08004

### 3.6 Web page / online document

> Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL

Example:
> World Health Organization. (2023, June). *Social isolation and loneliness among older people: Advocacy brief*. WHO. https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240080730

If author is the same as site name, omit site name.

### 3.7 Software / artifact (when the artifact is the contribution)

> Aboammar, M. (2026). *Rafiq-Bot: Open-source AI companion runtime* [Software]. GitHub. https://github.com/imMamdouhaboammar/Rafiq-Bot

For design-science research, the artifact (Rafiq) gets a citation entry. Software z.citation typically: title italic + `(Software)` in brackets + source URL.

### 3.8 Dissertation / thesis

> Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of dissertation* [Doctoral dissertation, Institution]. ProQuest. (URL if available)

### 3.9 Reports / grey literature

> Organization Name. (Year). *Title of report* (Report No. xxx). Publisher. URL

## 4. Special cases

- **Up to 20 authors**: list all. **21+ authors**: list first 19, then ellipsis, then last author
- **Multiple works by same author in same year**: 2020a, 2020b, etc.
- **No date**: `(n.d.)` in parenthetical; `(n.d.)` at end of reference entry
- **In press**: `(in press)` parenthetical; "Manuscript submitted for publication" note in reference entry
- **Reprints**: cite the original work; reference list entry uses original publication year
- **Preprint vs. published**: cite the published version when available; cite preprint only if it is what you actually consulted

---

## 5. Tables and figures

- **Numbered with Arabic numerals** in the order they appear
- **Tables:** Caption ABOVE. Reference in text BEFORE the table
- **Figures:** Caption BELOW. Reference in text BEFORE the figure
- **Tables/Figures integrated** with text — never at the end as a separate appendix unless explicitly told
- **Permission** required to reproduce any previously published figure

## 6. Common errors per the APA manual (top 15)

| # | Error | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missing year in in-text citation | Add year |
| 2 | Comma before year in narrative citation | Use `Author (Year)` not `Author, Year` |
| 3 | `&` in narrative citation | Use "and" in narrative, `&` in parenthetical |
| 4 | Forgetting "et al." rule for 3+ authors | Use first author et al. in all subsequent in-text citations |
| 5 | Quotation mark missing for short quote | Wrap direct quote in double quotes |
| 6 | Block quote formatted as inline | Indent 0.5 in, omit quotes |
| 7 | Title-case throughout | Use sentence case for titles in references |
| 8 | DOI presented as URL | `https://doi.org/xxxxx` — not dx.doi.org or doi: form |
| 9 | Page ranges abbreviated | `127–135` not `127-35` |
| 10 | Period inside vs. outside quotes | In American English: period and comma go inside; all other punctuation outside |
| 11 | Missing hanging indent in references | Apply 0.5-in hanging indent |
| 12 | Wrong year for reprint/copyright date | Use the original publication year |
| 13 | Mis-citing secondary source | Cite the original (e.g., Bowlby 1969) not the citing review |
| 14 | Mixing styles in the reference list | Single style end-to-end |
| 15 | Missing running head (professional paper) | Use page header with shortened title, ≤50 chars |

## 7. Style consistency checklist (for QA)

- [ ] Every in-text citation has a matching reference entry
- [ ] Every reference entry is cited in text
- [ ] All authors spelled the same way across the manuscript
- [ ] All journals italicized in references
- [ ] All DOIs as `https://doi.org/xxxx` (new APA 7 format)
- [ ] All URLs in references are live and correct as of submission date
- [ ] All in-text citations include year
- [ ] All direct quotes include page or paragraph
- [ ] Reference list starts on a new page with heading "References" (centered, bold, level-1 heading style)
- [ ] Software artifact cited with `(Software)` after title and version
- [ ] arXiv preprints cited with `arXiv` italicized where treated as repository
- [ ] Tables numbered with Arabic numerals in order, captioned above
- [ ] Figures numbered with Arabic numerals in order, captioned below
- [ ] Tables/figures referenced in text before appearance

## 8. Headings (APA 7 levels)

| Level | Format |
|---|---|
| 1 | Centered, Bold, Title Case |
| 2 | Flush Left, Bold, Title Case |
| 3 | Flush Left, Bold Italic, Title Case |
| 4 | Indented, Bold, Title Case, Period. Text begins on same line. |
| 5 | Indented, Bold Italic, Title Case, Period. Text begins on same line. |

Use exactly five levels; do not skip.
