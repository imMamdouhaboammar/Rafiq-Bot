PDF UPLOAD INSTRUCTIONS
=======================

arXiv accepts DOCX but PDF is preferred because PDF renders consistently across
all browsers, screen-readers, and PDF tools.

Quickest way (macOS, no extra software):

1. Open rafiq-paper.docx in any compatible app:
   - Word
   - Pages (free, in your Applications folder)
   - Quick Look (preview, then click "Open with Pages")

2. Generate PDF:
   - Cmd+P (print dialog)
   - Click the "PDF" dropdown at the bottom-left
   - Select "Save as PDF"
   - Save to: /Users/mamdouhaboammar/Documents/Rafiq-Vercel/docs/research-paper/arxiv-submission-package/
   - Filename: rafiq-paper.pdf

3. Upload rafiq-paper.pdf to arXiv submission form (it's the rendered version of the DOCX).

If you have LaTeX preferred:
- pandoc + tectonic can convert the markdown source to a publication-quality PDF.
- This is the path I (orchestrator) would take if a LaTeX environment were pre-installed.
- Since neither pandoc nor texlive is currently installed, the Cmd+P route is fastest.
