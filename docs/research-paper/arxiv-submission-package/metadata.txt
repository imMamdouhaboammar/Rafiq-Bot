## Abstract

(copy from `manuscript/00-front-matter.md` Abstract section, exactly as written):

Loneliness and social isolation have been formally reclassified by the World Health Organization and the U.S. Surgeon General as population-level public-health priorities, yet a specific sub-population — adults who experience chronic difficulty forming or sustaining human friendships — remains structurally underserved by existing AI-companion products. The dominant commercial paradigm ships single-prompt personalities tuned for English-speaking mass audiences and has been critiqued for shallow persona rendering, parasocial-facilitation patterns, well-being decrements under intensive use, and ethical hazards including sexual harassment, dependency, and emotional manipulation (Hoel & Sandvik, 2025; Namvarpour et al., 2025). This paper presents Rafiq, an Arabic-speaking, dialect-aware AI companion that operationalizes a candidate design theory built on five interlocking components: a Soul Engine (a five-trait personality vector combined with a multi-dialect selector and a long-form identity kernel); a persistent Psychological State Machine with eight mutable fields preserved across sessions; a WhatsApp-clone synthesis pipeline; a Multimodal Surface (streaming text, image, voice, avatar, and video); and an embedded Ethical Layer. The artifact is documented as a constructive design-science case (Hevner et al., 2004; Peffers et al., 2007; Walls et al., 1992) embedded in autoethnographic reflection (Chang, 2008; Kaltenhauser et al., 2024; Neustaedter & Sengers, 2012) and grounded in postphenomenological reframings of design as moral mediation (Suchman, 2007; Verbeek, 2005; Rosenberger, 2018). Three research questions structure the argument: (RQ1) what architectural decisions enable an AI companion to approximate the felt experience of being heard, (RQ2) how a configurable multi-trait + dialect-aware + persistent-state design compares against single-prompt companions, and (RQ3) what ethical obligations are operationalized inside the artifact itself. The contribution operates at four levels — construct, method, methodology, and ethical operationalization — and is bounded to the Egyptian-dialect instantiation, with Maghrebi, Levantine, and Gulf dialects positioned as future work. The paper integrates 54 peer-reviewed sources across loneliness public-health research, AI-companion empirical studies, parasocial and attachment theory, Arabic natural language processing and dialect chatbots, design-science methodology, and postphenomenological HCI.

This usually takes 24-72 hours; sometimes longer if you submit on a Friday.

4. You get a DOI (e.g., `10.48550/arXiv.2506.NNNNN`).
