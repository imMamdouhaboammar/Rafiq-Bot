## Working thesis

**Rafiq: A Design Case Study of a Configurable, Dialect-Aware AI Companion for the Socially Isolated**

A constructive design-science case study that uses Rafiq — an Arabic-speaking, dialect-aware, multimodal AI companion built around a Soul Engine + persistent psychological-state machine — as the artifact for arguing that *configurable personality*, *dialect authenticity*, and *state persistence*, taken together, are design moves that approximate the felt experience of being heard, in a population underserved by existing companion-AI designs.

## Methodological framing

- **Design Science Research** (Hevner et al., 2004; Peffers et al., 2008) for artifact evaluation
- **Autoethnographic reflection** (Chang, 2008; Kaltenhauser et al., 2024) for the designer's first-person voice
- **Single-developer constructive case** (no user studies at this stage)
