# Soul Mixer Upgrade — Superseded

This proposal was superseded by [Living Persona Core](./living-persona-core.md).

The manual sliders, recipes, and randomizer were removed because their precision was misleading and most dimensions did not affect the normal streaming-chat path. Existing `soulTraits` remain an internal compatibility baseline for previously created and imported personas.

Do not reintroduce direct numeric personality controls without evidence that users can predict their behavioral effect across direct chat, streaming, groups, and initiative messages.
